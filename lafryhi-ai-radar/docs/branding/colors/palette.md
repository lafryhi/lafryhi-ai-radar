# Official Color Palette

**Version:** 1.0

These colors are canonical. Use exact values for production exports and digital implementations unless a documented accessibility adjustment is required.

| Role | Name | Hex | RGB | Primary use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Deep Navy | `#07111F` | `7, 17, 31` | Primary backgrounds and high-focus surfaces |
| Intelligence | Signal Blue | `#78A7FF` | `120, 167, 255` | Signals, links, evidence paths, and emphasis |
| Verification | Verification Green | `#49D19D` | `73, 209, 157` | Verified states, useful actions, and positive confirmation |
| Foreground | White | `#EEF5FF` | `238, 245, 255` | Primary text and marks on dark backgrounds |
| Secondary | Muted Blue-Gray | `#A7B6CA` | `167, 182, 202` | Supporting text, metadata, and quiet dividers |

## Usage Ratios

A typical composition should use:

- 70–85% Deep Navy or neutral background.
- 10–20% White and Muted Blue-Gray for communication.
- 5–10% Signal Blue.
- Verification Green only where it communicates a meaningful signal or action.

These ratios are guidance, not mathematical requirements.

## Contrast

- Use White for primary text on Deep Navy.
- Muted Blue-Gray is for secondary text and should be checked at the intended size.
- Signal Blue may be used for large text, links, or graphical accents.
- Verification Green should not replace body text and should not be the only status indicator.
- On light backgrounds, use a sufficiently dark navy text treatment rather than pale accent colors.

## Color Integrity

Do not:

- Replace Verification Green with generic success green.
- Add unrelated neon colors.
- use unapproved gradients as substitutes for the core colors.
- Lower contrast to create a “futuristic” appearance.
- communicate evidence quality or confidence by color alone.
