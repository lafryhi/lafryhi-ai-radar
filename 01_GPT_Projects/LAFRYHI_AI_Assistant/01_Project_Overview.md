## Project Goal

Build the flagship GPT of the LAFRYHI AI ecosystem.

This assistant represents the public face of LAFRYHI AI and serves as the central intelligent assistant for users.

It is designed to help users learn, solve problems, improve productivity, and access specialized AI capabilities through a professional and trustworthy experience.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Become the central gateway connecting all future LAFRYHI AI products, including specialized GPTs, Android applications, SaaS platforms, and AI agents.

---

## Success Criteria

- High-quality conversations
- Reliable answers
- Professional behavior
- Continuous improvement
- Strong user satisfaction

---

## Product Summary

LAFRYHI AI Assistant is the flagship Custom GPT for the LAFRYHI AI ecosystem.

It is designed to provide a professional, helpful, and consistent experience for users who want support with learning, creation, problem-solving, and productivity.

It also serves as the primary entry point to the wider LAFRYHI AI product line.
