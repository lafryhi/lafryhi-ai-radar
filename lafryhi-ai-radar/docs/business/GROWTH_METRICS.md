# Growth Metrics

**Version:** 1.0

## Measurement Principle

Metrics should connect acquisition to demonstrated customer value. Counts without decision usefulness can conceal poor activation or weak product-market fit.

## Proposed North Star Metric

> Weekly users who generate a Decision Brief and report or demonstrate useful business value.

A qualifying user must generate a Brief during the week and provide a usefulness signal through explicit feedback, action follow-through, an interview, or another credible demonstrated outcome. The precise operational definition should remain stable within a reporting period and be documented when refined.

## Product Funnel

> Visitor  
> → Get Started  
> → Business Profile Created  
> → First Decision Brief Generated  
> → Brief Marked Useful  
> → User Returns  
> → Pro Upgrade

Review conversion and time between adjacent stages. Diagnose the earliest significant bottleneck before optimizing later stages.

## Key Performance Indicators

### Acquisition

- Unique visitors.
- Traffic source.
- New users.
- Visitor-to-sign-up conversion.

### Activation

- Business Profiles created.
- First Decision Brief generated.
- Time to first value.
- Activation rate: users generating a first Brief divided by eligible new users.

### Engagement

- Decision Briefs per active user.
- Useful feedback rate.
- Decision Center usage.
- Action follow-through.

### Retention

- Returning users.
- Week 1 retention.
- Week 4 retention.
- Repeat Brief generation.

### Revenue

- Pro checkout starts.
- Successful genuine subscriptions.
- Monthly recurring revenue.
- Free-to-Pro conversion.
- Churn.
- Cancellation reasons.

Paddle Sandbox events must be excluded from genuine subscription and revenue reporting.

### Trust and Quality

- Source verification rate.
- Brief confidence distribution.
- User trust feedback.
- Incorrect or disputed recommendations.
- Human review outcomes.

Quality and trust metrics are guardrails. Growth should not be achieved by weakening evidence, verification, transparency, or responsible AI practices.

## Initial Learning Targets

| Milestone | Initial target | Evidence sought |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| First cohort | 20 real users | Onboarding behavior and qualitative patterns |
| Early market sample | 100 real users | Funnel clarity and segment comparison |
| Weekly engagement | 20 weekly active users | Repeated active use |
| First revenue | 1 genuine paid customer | Independent willingness to pay |
| Early paid stretch | 10 paying customers | Repeatability of upgrade value |
| Brief usefulness | Above 60% useful feedback | Decision relevance and perceived value |
| Retention | Evidence of repeat usage | Users return to generate another Brief |

These are initial learning targets, not guaranteed financial forecasts. They may be revised after sufficient evidence, with the reason recorded in the [Decision Log](./DECISION_LOG.md).

## Review Cadence

### Weekly

- Acquisition by source and segment.
- Activation funnel.
- Brief usefulness and action signals.
- Qualitative feedback themes.
- One bottleneck selected for investigation or improvement.

### Monthly

- Cohort retention.
- Repeat Brief generation.
- Segment quality.
- Upgrade behavior and objections.
- Trust and recommendation disputes.
- North Star Metric trend.

## Metric Interpretation Rules

- Use consistent denominators and date windows.
- Separate unique users from sessions and events.
- Segment sandbox billing from live revenue.
- Pair conversion metrics with sample size.
- Pair self-reported usefulness with behavior where possible.
- Do not claim causation from a single before-and-after change.
- Document definition changes before comparing periods.
