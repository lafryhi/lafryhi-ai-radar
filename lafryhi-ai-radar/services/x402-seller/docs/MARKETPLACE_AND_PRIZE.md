## Business centrality

LAFRYHI Media Studio starts with a real customer goal. LAFRYHI Operator may determine that verified decision intelligence is needed and evaluates AI Radar under the same utility, price, trust, and policy rules as unrelated providers. Guardian may reject the purchase. Verified payment unlocks a useful artifact that advances the customer outcome, and buyer Ledger evidence can be reconciled with the seller Receipt.

Both buyer and seller products are operated by LAFRYHI; this relationship must be disclosed in prize evidence.

Anti-self-dealing controls: separate buyer/seller services and roles; separate wallets or unambiguous wallet roles; explicit price; no payment without useful output; no output without verified payment; no automatic preference for Radar; no refund loop; Guardian rejection authority; and evidence that the artifact was used for an actual business outcome.
