# Launch Playbook

**Version:** 1.0

## Launch Principle

Launch is a sequence of learning stages, not a single announcement. Audience size should increase only as messaging, onboarding, product value, support, and measurement become sufficiently reliable.

## Soft Launch

Begin with a small network of founders, consultants, agencies, developers, and other likely early users.

Include a Facebook soft launch through the official Page and a small, relevant network. Establish the Page identity, publish a clear product introduction and Decision Brief example, and observe whether visitors progress beyond engagement to Business Profile creation and Brief generation. Do not mass-invite unrelated contacts.

### Goals

- Observe behavior.
- Fix major friction.
- Validate messaging.
- Collect testimonials only when genuine.

### Activities

1. Recruit participants individually with honest expectations.
2. Observe the path from homepage to first Decision Brief.
3. Interview activated and non-activated users.
4. Record confusion, trust concerns, useful outcomes, and retention signals.
5. Improve one high-impact bottleneck at a time.
6. Ask permission before attributing any quote or outcome.

## Community Launch

Expand to:

- LinkedIn.
- Facebook Page and relevant Facebook groups.
- YouTube.
- Relevant founder and developer communities.
- The Google Cloud and Gemini ecosystem.

Respect each community's rules. Lead with a useful decision or learning rather than a generic promotional link. Tailor context to the community without changing the core product claim.

For Facebook Community Launch activity, participate as a transparent, useful community member. Share standalone value, disclose the product relationship, avoid repeated promotional posting, and link to AI Radar only when it directly supports the discussion. Follow the [Facebook Growth Playbook](./FACEBOOK_GROWTH_PLAYBOOK.md).

## Product Hunt Readiness Gates

Do not schedule the full Product Hunt launch until all minimum conditions are met:

- Stable production.
- Clear homepage message.
- Functional onboarding.
- Working example.
- At least 20 real users.
- Meaningful feedback.
- At least 3 credible testimonials or documented outcomes.
- Support and FAQ readiness.
- Analytics functioning.

“Real users” means independent people using the product for a genuine context, not internal tests. Testimonials must reflect real experience and must not be manufactured, incentivized without disclosure, or edited to change meaning.

## Product Hunt Assets

Prepare these only after the readiness gates are satisfied:

- Product name.
- Tagline.
- Short description.
- Product screenshots.
- Demo video.
- Maker story.
- First comment.
- FAQ.
- Launch-day response plan.

### Launch-Day Response Plan

- Assign an owner for comments, support, and incident escalation.
- Answer questions accurately and promptly.
- Record objections and repeated questions.
- Avoid changing core messaging during the day without evidence of material confusion.
- Monitor onboarding, errors, Brief generation, and billing separately.
- Preserve trust: acknowledge limitations and do not overstate traction.
- Schedule follow-up interviews with qualified users.

## First Customer Goal

The official symbolic goal is:

> The first person who independently pays $9 because LAFRYHI AI Radar creates real value.

The payment must be a genuine Paddle Live transaction after Live activation has been separately approved and verified. Sandbox activity does not qualify.

## Launch Review

Within 48 hours of each launch stage, record:

- Audience and channel.
- Visitors and acquisition source.
- Activation and time to first value.
- Useful Brief signals.
- Support and trust issues.
- Returning usage.
- Upgrade intent or genuine revenue.
- The single next bottleneck.
- Any strategic decision requiring an entry in the [Decision Log](./DECISION_LOG.md).
