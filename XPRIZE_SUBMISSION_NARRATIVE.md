# XPRIZE Submission Narrative — LAFRYHI AI Radar

LAFRYHI AI Radar is an AI decision-intelligence system designed for small businesses that need useful recommendations without giving up evidence, control, or accountability. The business problem is straightforward: a small-business owner can face more technology, market, operational, and competitive information than they can evaluate consistently. Radar turns governed source material into structured Decision Briefs that explain what changed, why it matters, what action is recommended, how confident the system is, and what evidence supports the recommendation.

AI is used in the day-to-day operating workflow, not as a decorative chatbot. Governed sources are retrieved with provenance and content controls, then Gemini is invoked through Vertex AI. In the production evidence captured on August 2, 2026, the configured production model was `gemini-2.5-flash`. Gemini performs the intelligence work: it produces structured business significance, opportunity and risk assessment, confidence, recommended actions, evidence selection, and warnings. The result is then parsed and checked against strict application schemas before it can move forward.

The system deliberately separates what AI does from what deterministic software and the human operator do. Application code controls source admission, bounded retrieval, duplicate detection, schema validation, workflow state, editorial policy, and publication gates. Gemini cannot simply declare its own output published. A Human Verification queue gives the operator the source provenance, evidence, confidence, conflicts, and recommendation. The operator approves or rejects the candidate and remains accountable for the consequential publication boundary. Only an approved record can become a Decision Brief in the Decision Center.

This separation is central to how we operate the business. We want AI to do high-value analytical work at machine speed while keeping important business actions traceable and governed. The production evidence captured a Cloud Run deployment in `us-central1`, Vertex AI Gemini execution, Firestore-backed records, Secret Manager-backed runtime secrets, Cloud Logging and Monitoring, health/readiness checks, and authenticated Human Verification states. At that capture point the Operator Dashboard showed completed analyses, items awaiting verification, verified analyses, and published Decision Briefs. These are dated production facts rather than simulated claims.

After the production evidence capture, the project added Proof v1 to demonstrate how an approved decision can cross into a separately bounded execution flow. Proof v1 connects a Decision Brief to a quote, a governance/authorization reference, settlement verification, fulfillment, and SHA-256 artifact verification. Its frozen fixture uses Arc Testnet and `0.01 USDC`, with transaction correlation, read-only Circle verification, Firestore-backed idempotency, and a frozen artifact digest. This is important technical evidence that the architecture can extend beyond analysis while retaining explicit boundaries. It is not commercial revenue, and we do not present testnet activity as a customer payment.

For small businesses, the economic opportunity is not simply “replace people with AI.” The stronger model is to create a reliable operating layer around owners and teams: source curation, business analysis, verification, implementation, customer success, integration, and domain-specific decision support. As the business grows, those functions can create work beyond the founder for reviewers and domain specialists, customer-success and onboarding roles, integration and automation specialists, source/governance operations, and sales or partnership roles. AI increases the leverage of those people by handling repetitive evidence synthesis while humans retain responsibility for context, trust, customer relationships, and consequential authorization.

The project has also been built with a strict evidence policy. We do not fabricate users, customers, testimonials, revenue, expenses, cloud executions, or AI runs. Google Cloud billing is treated as cost evidence, not revenue. Testnet USDC is treated as execution evidence, not revenue. Final revenue, expense, marketing-spend, P&L, and customer evidence supplied to the competition must come from genuine records; if a genuine value is zero, it should be reported as zero rather than replaced with a more impressive invented number. Private customer contact details and sensitive financial records should be supplied through the appropriate judging channel instead of being published in the repository.

LAFRYHI AI Radar belongs in Small Business Services because its purpose is operational: help a small business convert a noisy stream of external information into evidence-backed decisions and, where explicitly authorized, bounded execution. The system combines Gemini’s analytical capability with Google Cloud production infrastructure, deterministic safeguards, human verification, and traceable execution. The result is an AI-operated workflow designed not merely to generate answers, but to support real business decisions while preserving the evidence and accountability needed to trust them.
