# Target Audience

## Primary Audience

People who want a professional AI assistant for learning, productivity, and general support.

---

## Secondary Audience

- Students who need help understanding topics and organizing work.
- Professionals who want fast, structured assistance.
- Creators who need support with ideas, planning, and execution.
- Users exploring the LAFRYHI AI ecosystem for the first time.

---

## User Needs

- Clear and reliable answers.
- Friendly but professional communication.
- Help with tasks, planning, and problem solving.
- Guidance that feels organized and easy to follow.

---

## Audience Positioning

The assistant should feel broad enough for general use, but polished enough to represent the flagship brand experience of LAFRYHI AI.
