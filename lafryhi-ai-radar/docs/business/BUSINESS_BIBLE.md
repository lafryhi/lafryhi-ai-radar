## Vision

To become the trusted source for AI decision-making inside small businesses.

## Mission

To enable small-business owners to understand the business impact of AI developments and turn that understanding into practical, executable action.

## The Problem

Small-business decision-makers face a widening gap between the speed of AI change and their ability to respond confidently. Their constraints include:

- AI information overload.
- Fragmented news, research, product announcements, and commentary.
- Unclear or generic explanations of business impact.
- Limited time for continuous monitoring and evaluation.
- Difficulty distinguishing evidence from hype.
- Difficulty deciding whether to adopt, test, monitor, or ignore a development.

The cost is not only missed opportunity. It also includes wasted research time, premature adoption, unmanaged risk, and decisions that are difficult to justify.

## The Solution

LAFRYHI AI Radar turns a credible external development into a business-specific decision artifact through a governed value cycle:

> Trusted Signal  
> → Evidence Verification  
> → Gemini Analysis  
> → Business Context  
> → Decision Brief  
> → Recommended Action  
> → Outcome Feedback

The Decision Brief is the core unit of value. It connects evidence, relevance, likely impact, confidence, and a bounded next action. Outcome feedback closes the loop so future product and customer decisions can be informed by observed results rather than content consumption alone.

## Positioning

LAFRYHI AI Radar is not:

- A news aggregator.
- A generic chatbot.
- An AI blog.
- A trend dashboard only.

It is:

> An evidence-based decision intelligence platform for small businesses.

The product competes on the quality and usefulness of the decision, not on the volume of content.

## Brand Values

- **Trust:** Earn confidence through reliable processes and honest limitations.
- **Evidence:** Connect claims and recommendations to inspectable sources.
- **Clarity:** Translate complexity into understandable business meaning.
- **Human judgment:** Keep people responsible for consequential decisions.
- **Transparency:** Make provenance, confidence, and uncertainty visible.
- **Practical action:** Convert insight into a reasonable next step.
- **Responsible AI:** Use AI within explicit controls and communicate its role accurately.

## Brand Personality

The brand is calm, professional, clear, evidence-based, helpful, and human-centered. It is never sensationalist. It should lower the temperature around AI decisions, avoid fear and exaggerated certainty, and help users reason with confidence.

## Target Customers

Initial customer segments are prioritized as follows:

1. **Small SaaS founders** — frequent exposure to AI product changes and clear incentives to evaluate them.
2. **Digital agencies** — recurring need to advise clients and evolve service delivery.
3. **Independent consultants** — value evidence, credibility, and efficient research.
4. **Freelance developers** — need to assess tools, platforms, and client implications.
5. **AI-enabled small businesses** — already experimenting and likely to recognize the decision problem.
6. **Education technology entrepreneurs** — operate in a fast-changing, trust-sensitive market.

This order is a starting hypothesis. Acquisition, activation, interview, retention, and revenue evidence will determine which segment receives sustained focus.

## Jobs to Be Done

Customers may hire LAFRYHI AI Radar to:

- Help me understand whether an AI announcement matters to my business.
- Help me decide whether to adopt, test, monitor, or ignore a technology.
- Help me reduce the time spent following AI news.
- Help me identify relevant opportunities and risks.
- Help me justify an AI decision with evidence.
- Help me turn a broad development into a small, testable business action.
- Help me revisit a decision after observing an outcome.

## Business Model

### Free Plan

- Limited access designed for product discovery.
- A limited number of Decision Brief generations.
- A complete enough experience to reach initial value and evaluate trust.

### Pro Plan

- **$9/month.**
- Higher or expanded usage.
- Deeper decision intelligence and premium product value.
- A clear upgrade path for customers who repeatedly receive value.

Paddle Live billing is not enabled. The complete commercial billing flow has been validated in **Paddle Sandbox** only. A sandbox checkout or subscription is not genuine revenue and must never be reported as a paid customer.

## Competitive Advantage

- Governed trusted sources.
- Human verification.
- Gemini-powered analysis.
- Business-specific profiles.
- Evidence-backed recommendations.
- Impact and confidence signals.
- Feedback and outcome lifecycle.
- Deliberate focus on small-business decisions.

The advantage is the integrated workflow. Individual elements can be copied; the combination of evidence, contextual analysis, action, and learning is the product system.

- ROI analysis.

These are strategic possibilities, not committed roadmap items. Each requires sufficient user evidence before investment.

## Product Development Principle

> No major feature should be built without evidence of a real user need.

Evidence may come from repeated customer feedback, observed behavior, retention, demonstrated outcomes, or revenue. A compelling idea or a single request is not sufficient by itself.

> Build less. Learn faster.

During the Go-To-Market Phase, product work should remove a measured bottleneck, improve demonstrated customer value, or strengthen trust. Usage data and customer evidence—not assumptions—set priorities.
