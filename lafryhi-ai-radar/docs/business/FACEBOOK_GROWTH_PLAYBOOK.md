# Facebook Growth Playbook

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-29

## 1. Purpose

Facebook is an official primary customer acquisition channel for LAFRYHI AI Radar because it provides direct access to a large and diverse small-business audience. Its communities include Arabic-speaking entrepreneurs, developers, freelancers, agencies, teachers interested in AI, local business owners, and startup operators who regularly exchange practical advice.

Facebook complements LinkedIn; it does not replace it. LinkedIn is well suited to professional positioning, founder networks, and business-to-business conversations. Facebook adds community-led discovery, local and regional reach, discussion groups, accessible educational formats, and particularly strong access to Arabic-speaking markets. Channel performance will be evaluated by qualified product behavior rather than audience size alone.

## 2. Strategic Goals

Facebook activity should:

- Contribute to acquiring the first 100 real users.
- Build trust through clear, evidence-based education.
- Demonstrate the value of a Decision Brief.
- Collect specific customer feedback and objections.
- Generate repeat visitors.
- Create useful community discussions.
- Drive Business Profile creation.
- Generate Decision Briefs.
- Identify audiences that demonstrate useful outcomes and return usage.

## 5. Content Pillars

- **AI Decision of the Week:** Explain one verified development and the decision it creates.
- **AI Opportunity of the Week:** Show a bounded opportunity for a relevant small-business segment.
- **Decision Brief Demo:** Demonstrate evidence, impact, confidence, and recommended action.
- **Behind the Build:** Explain responsible product choices and learning.
- **Founder Notes:** Share honest Go-To-Market observations without manufacturing traction.
- **Gemini Insights:** Explain how Gemini-supported analysis contributes to decision intelligence without implying Google endorsement.
- **AI Myth vs Reality:** Separate verified facts from common assumptions.
- **Customer Success Stories:** Share genuine, permission-based outcomes only.
- **Product Updates:** Explain meaningful improvements in terms of customer value.

## 6. Content Formats

Use the format that best supports the idea:

- Images.
- Carousels.
- Native videos.
- Short videos.
- Polls.
- Stories.
- Live sessions.
- Infographics.
- Decision Brief screenshots.
- Founder posts.

Decision Brief screenshots must use demonstration data or properly authorized, anonymized customer material. Native formats should provide useful context even when the reader does not click.

## 7. Posting Schedule

Use a sustainable three-post weekly rhythm:

| Day | Post |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | AI Decision of the Week |
| Wednesday | Decision Brief Demo |
| Friday | Founder Update |
| Optional weekend | Educational post based on a demonstrated audience question |

Quality is more important than quantity. Do not publish filler to satisfy a schedule. Reuse a core weekly theme across LinkedIn and Facebook only when it is adapted to the audience and format of each channel.

## 8. Facebook Groups Strategy

Participate respectfully in relevant groups. The goal is to become a useful contributor, not to distribute links.

- Read and follow each group's rules.
- Never spam or post the same promotion across groups.
- Provide value before asking for attention.
- Share useful evidence, explanations, frameworks, and answers.
- Link to LAFRYHI AI Radar only when it is genuinely relevant to the discussion.
- Disclose the relationship to the product when mentioning it.
- Avoid direct messages unless invited or clearly appropriate.

Relevant communities may focus on:

- AI.
- SaaS.
- Entrepreneurs.
- Small business.
- Google Cloud.
- Developers.
- Education technology.

Track conversations that reveal recurring decision problems, trust requirements, or useful customer segments. Treat comments as discovery signals, not sufficient proof for major product development.

## 10. Community Guidelines

- Be helpful.
- Use no clickbait.
- Make no exaggerated claims.
- Respect discussions and community rules.
- Protect customer and community-member privacy.
- Never attack competitors.
- Always separate facts from opinions and interpretation.
- Correct material errors transparently.
- State uncertainty when evidence is incomplete.
- Avoid presenting Gemini or Google Cloud usage as endorsement.

## 11. Success Metrics

Track:

- Facebook followers.
- Reach.
- Engagement.
- Link clicks.
- Business Profiles created from Facebook traffic.
- Decision Briefs generated by Facebook-acquired users.
- Returning users.
- Genuine paid conversions.
- Meaningful conversations.

Followers, reach, and engagement are diagnostic metrics. Business Profile creation, Decision Brief generation, usefulness, return usage, and genuine paid conversion are stronger evidence of customer value. Paddle Sandbox events must not be counted as revenue.

## 12. First 30-Day Plan

| Week | Focus | Activities | Expected outputs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Launch the Page | Complete profile assets, publish the product introduction, Decision Brief explanation, and first evidence-led post; invite a small relevant network. | Complete official presence, baseline audience data, initial questions and reactions. |
| 2 | Educational content | Publish the three-post rhythm and test image, carousel, and native-video formats. | Early format comparison, link-click baseline, recurring audience questions. |
| 3 | Community participation | Contribute useful answers in relevant groups, follow community rules, and link only when relevant. | Meaningful conversations, qualified visits, initial segment and trust signals. |
| 4 | Analyze performance | Review source traffic, profiles, Briefs, return behavior, feedback, and content quality; interview responsive users where possible. | 30-day evidence summary, winning themes, weak assumptions, and one prioritized improvement. |

The first month is an experiment. It does not guarantee a fixed number of users. Its purpose is to establish whether Facebook can attract people who reach and repeat meaningful product value.

## 13. Risks and Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Low engagement | Test audience-specific problems, formats, and posting times; prioritize qualified conversations over vanity metrics. |
| Wrong audience | Compare downstream activation and usefulness by community and content theme; narrow focus based on behavior. |
| Spam perception | Follow group rules, disclose affiliation, provide standalone value, and link sparingly. |
| Poor consistency | Maintain the sustainable three-post cadence and prepare a weekly content brief in advance. |
| Low conversion | Diagnose the path from click to Business Profile and first Decision Brief; improve one measured bottleneck rather than increasing posting volume. |

## 14. Long-Term Vision

Facebook can become:

- A community around practical AI decisions.
- An education channel for evidence-based AI adoption.
- A source of customer feedback.
- A product-validation environment.
- A trust-building platform.
- A referral engine.

This vision depends on repeated evidence of qualified acquisition, useful Decision Briefs, return usage, and customer outcomes. The channel should expand only when it supports those outcomes responsibly.
