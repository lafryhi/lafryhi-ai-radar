# Content Strategy

**Version:** 1.0

## Objective

Content should help small-business decision-makers understand a real AI development, see how evidence becomes action, and decide whether LAFRYHI AI Radar is relevant to their work. It is an acquisition and trust-building system, not a publishing volume contest.

## Channels

### Primary Channel

**LinkedIn**

LinkedIn provides direct access to founders, consultants, agency leaders, freelance professionals, and small-business decision-makers. It supports short evidence-led posts, practical examples, founder learning, and direct conversations.

### Secondary Channels

- YouTube.
- Product Hunt.
- Dev.to.
- Medium.
- Google Cloud and Gemini communities.

Secondary channels should reuse or deepen validated themes rather than create separate high-volume editorial obligations.

## Content Pillars

| Pillar | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| AI Decision of the Week | Explain one meaningful development and the decision it creates. |
| AI Opportunity of the Week | Show a bounded opportunity for a small-business segment. |
| Decision Brief Demo | Demonstrate evidence, business impact, confidence, and action. |
| Behind the Build | Explain responsible product choices and lessons from building the platform. |
| Founder Notes | Share honest Go-To-Market learning and customer evidence. |
| Evidence versus Hype | Separate verified facts from interpretation and speculation. |
| Small Business AI Action Guide | Offer practical frameworks for adopt, test, monitor, or ignore decisions. |

## Weekly Publishing Rhythm

Use a sustainable cadence of three posts per week:

| Day | Format | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | AI Decision of the Week | Earn attention through a timely, evidence-led business decision. |
| Wednesday | Decision Brief Demo | Make the product's value concrete. |
| Friday | Behind the Build or Founder Insight | Build trust through transparent learning and responsible practice. |

A post should be repurposed for a secondary channel only when the format adds value—for example, a deeper YouTube walkthrough or a Dev.to technical explanation.

## Calls to Action

Use one clear, relevant call to action:

> See what this AI development means for your business.

> Create your business profile and generate a free Decision Brief.

Avoid stacking multiple asks. Conversation-oriented posts may instead invite readers to describe the last AI decision they found difficult.

## Content Rules

- Make no unsupported claims.
- Use no exaggerated language.
- Create no fake urgency.
- Separate facts from interpretation.
- Link value to a business action.
- Protect confidential data.
- Use real product examples without exposing user information.
- Link to primary evidence when discussing a signal.
- State uncertainty and limitations when material.
- Never present Paddle Sandbox activity as real revenue or live billing.
- Never imply endorsement by Google, Gemini, or Google Cloud.

## Learning Loop

Track which themes generate qualified conversations, profile creation, first Briefs, useful outcomes, and return usage. Do not optimize for impressions alone. Feed repeated questions and objections into customer discovery, not directly into major feature development.
