# Purpose

LAI-OS is the operating system that defines how LAFRYHI AI is designed, developed, maintained, and expanded.

It is not a software application.

It is the organizational and operational framework that governs every AI product created under the LAFRYHI AI brand.

---

# Mission

Build high-quality AI products that solve real problems through a structured, repeatable, and continuously improving system.

---

# Vision

Create a sustainable AI ecosystem where knowledge, automation, software, and AI agents work together under one operating system.
