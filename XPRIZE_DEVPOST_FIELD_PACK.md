## Public project description

LAFRYHI AI Radar is an evidence-driven AI decision-intelligence system for small businesses. Governed source material is retrieved with provenance controls and analyzed by Gemini on Vertex AI. Gemini produces structured business significance, opportunity/risk assessment, confidence, recommended actions, evidence selection, and warnings. Deterministic application code validates schemas, enforces policy and workflow invariants, and prevents model output from publishing itself. A human operator reviews the evidence and approves or rejects consequential publication/action boundaries. Approved records become Decision Briefs; Proof v1 demonstrates a separately bounded testnet execution and verified fulfillment path.

The project fits Small Business Services because it helps everyday businesses turn noisy external information into evidence-backed decisions while preserving traceability and human accountability.

## AI-native operations

**Gemini:** evidence synthesis, structured analysis, opportunity/risk assessment, confidence, recommended actions, evidence selection, warnings.

**Deterministic software:** source admission, bounded retrieval, duplicate detection, schema validation, policy, state transitions, publication gates, authorization correlation, idempotency, settlement verification, artifact hashing.

**Human:** reviews provenance/evidence/recommendation and authorizes consequential publication/action boundaries.
