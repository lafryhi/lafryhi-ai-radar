# LAFRYHI AI Radar Brand Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-29

## Brand Foundation

| Element | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Product name | LAFRYHI AI Radar |
| Category | Decision Intelligence Platform |
| Audience | Small Businesses |
| Tagline | Decision Intelligence for Small Businesses |
| Core promise | Transform Trusted AI Signals into Confident Business Decisions. |

The identity should communicate trust, evidence, clarity, human judgment, transparency, practical action, and responsible AI. Its tone is calm, professional, clear, evidence-based, helpful, and human-centered—never sensationalist.

## Official References

The files in [`reference/`](./reference/) are the single source of truth for visual identity. The consolidated [`06-brand-kit-reference.png`](./reference/06-brand-kit-reference.png) is the quickest overview; the individual references govern their respective formats.

These references define visual intent. Production exports placed in `logos/` or `social/` must be derived from, reviewed against, and visually consistent with them.

## Logo System

### Primary Lockup

The primary lockup combines:

- An abstract radar structure representing continuous monitoring.
- Signal points representing trusted developments and evidence.
- A forward decision motif representing practical action.
- The wordmark **LAFRYHI AI Radar**.
- The descriptor **Decision Intelligence for Small Businesses** when space permits.

Use [`01-logo.png`](./reference/01-logo.png) as the authoritative lockup reference.

### Standalone Mark

Use the standalone mark only where the primary wordmark cannot remain legible, including profile images, favicons, and compact avatars. Use [`02-profile-picture.png`](./reference/02-profile-picture.png) as the authoritative reference.

### Clear Space

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to at least the diameter of the smallest central signal point in the mark. No text, edge, photograph, or interface control should enter this area.

### Minimum Size

- Primary digital lockup: do not render below 220 px wide.
- Standalone mark: do not render below 24 px square.
- Descriptor: remove it rather than allowing it to become illegible.

### Incorrect Use

Do not:

- Stretch or compress the logo.
- Rotate, mirror, crop, or rearrange the mark.
- Change individual brand colors.
- Add outlines, bevels, drop shadows, or unapproved glow.
- Place it on visually noisy backgrounds without sufficient separation.
- Substitute another typeface inside the lockup.
- recreate the mark approximately from memory.
- Combine it with Google, Gemini, or third-party marks.

## Color System

The visual identity uses a restrained dark foundation with precise signal and verification accents:

- Deep Navy represents focus, stability, and decision context.
- Signal Blue represents monitored intelligence and evidence pathways.
- Verification Green represents confirmation, useful action, and human-verified confidence.
- White provides clear high-contrast communication.
- Muted Blue-Gray supports secondary information.

Canonical values and accessibility guidance are maintained in the [Color Palette](./colors/palette.md).

## Typography

Typography must be direct, legible, and modern without becoming futuristic or decorative. Use the approved type hierarchy and fallbacks in [Typography](./typography/fonts.md).

## Visual Language

Approved supporting motifs include:

- Radar rings and partial arcs.
- Focused signal points.
- Evidence pathways and connecting lines.
- Verification marks.
- Directional movement toward a clear outcome.
- Structured panels with generous negative space.

Use motifs sparingly. They support meaning and hierarchy; they are not decorative noise.

Avoid robots, brains, lightbulbs, generic network globes, exaggerated neon effects, stock “AI” imagery, and unsupported data visualizations.

### Social Posts

Use [`05-social-post-template.png`](./reference/05-social-post-template.png) as the master editorial layout:

1. Identify the decision topic.
2. Explain what changed.
3. Explain why it matters.
4. State what to do next.

Do not allow templates to manufacture certainty. Separate verified facts from interpretation and action.

## Image and Screenshot Standards

- Use genuine product examples or clearly identified demonstrations.
- Protect confidential and personal data.
- Obtain permission before using customer material.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, outcomes, metrics, or interface states.
- Crop screenshots intentionally and ensure text remains legible.
- Use overlays only when they improve comprehension.

## Accessibility

- Target WCAG AA contrast for body text and interactive elements.
- Never communicate status by color alone.
- Add meaningful alternative text when images convey information.
- Preserve safe margins for platform crops and zoom.
- Avoid small text embedded in raster assets where HTML text is available.
- Provide reduced-motion alternatives for animated brand treatments.

## Technology Attribution

LAFRYHI AI Radar may accurately state that it is powered by Gemini or built with Google Cloud where context requires. Such notices are technology attribution, not part of the LAFRYHI logo. They must not imply affiliation, certification, sponsorship, or endorsement.

## Governance

Brand changes require:

1. A clear use case or evidence of a real consistency problem.
2. Review against the Business Bible and existing references.
3. Replacement or addition of all affected references and exports.
4. Link and structure validation.
5. An entry in [`CHANGELOG.md`](./CHANGELOG.md).

Do not overwrite an official reference without recording the version change.
